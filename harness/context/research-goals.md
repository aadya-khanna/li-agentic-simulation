# Research goals

This project studies **multi-agent social behaviour under environmental pressure**, not roleplay fidelity.

## Independent variable

The **simulation design**: schedule, ceremonies, asymmetric information, prize framing, dump rules.

## Dependent variables (from logs)

- Public speech vs private `thought` vs strategic `play` (dual-track divergence)
- Who initiates contact; talk graph from `contacts`
- Recoupling picks when couples already exist (loyalty vs game)
- Save votes vs stated loyalty in diary/thoughts
- Reputation trajectory vs coupling survival

## Confounds we removed on purpose

- Character bibles (occupation, archetype, secrets, private goals)
- Numeric relationship matrices (trust/attraction/threat)
- Host-enforced "stealing" morality (TAKEN copy, gender-filtered pick pools)

## Hypothesis

Agent judgement and action are shaped more by **accumulated context + hard ceremony rules** than by pre-assigned personality. The harness exists to keep the codebase aligned with that experimental design.
